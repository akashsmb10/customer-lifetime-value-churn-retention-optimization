The actual top-20% share is 77.248485%; no forced Pareto rule is needed. The cumulative concentration curve asks whether a few customers dominate commercial exposure. Those observations motivate robust summaries and value-model backtests rather than automatic removal of wholesalers. Country totals describe coverage, not country-caused churn. Month-to-month growth combines cohort acquisition, repeat purchases and seasonal effects; it is not causal evidence of marketing performance.
